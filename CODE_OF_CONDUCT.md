# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## The standard

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In practice that means:

**Expected**

- Give and take feedback gracefully. A review that finds a problem is a
  contribution, not an attack.
- Be patient with people who are new — to this library, to React Native, or to
  graphics. Everyone was.
- Say what you mean plainly, and prefer a concrete suggestion to a vague
  complaint.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

Applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **mowglijuddi@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's safety comes before the project's convenience.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this document,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
